Privacy Policy

Commitments  ·  Effective Date: July 29, 2026  ·  Last Revised: July 29, 2026

Commitments (“the App,” “we,” “us”) is a personal planning application that helps users track tasks, manage deadlines, schedule work sessions, and optionally synchronize with Google Calendar. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used and stored, and what rights you have with respect to your data.

Please read this policy before creating an account. By registering for or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to its terms.

Short version: Your task data lives in a private, encrypted database accessible only to your account. Your Google Calendar data is used only to calculate your availability and write work blocks you explicitly request — it is never stored on our servers, shared, or used for any other purpose.

1. Who We Are

Commitments is developed and operated by Jampack Studios. Questions or data requests may be directed to: github.com/jbrownsberger.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address. Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth and supports two methods: a passwordless “magic link” sent to your email, or a traditional email-and-password combination. If you choose the password method, your password is hashed and stored by Supabase; we never see or store it in plaintext.

2b. Task and Planning Data

All data you enter is stored in our Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and associated with your unique user ID. This includes:

  • Tasks — name, status, priority, due date, estimated hours, notes, progress percentage, and recurrence configuration (cadence, schedule, series bounds)
  • Substeps — text, completion state, and weight within a parent task
  • Quick Tasks — name, duration estimate, deadline text, and completion status
  • Categories — name and color used to organize tasks
  • Scheduled Days — specific calendar dates on which you have planned to work on a task
  • User Preferences — display settings such as dark mode, default weekly working hours, and default session length

2c. Google Calendar Data (Optional)

You may optionally connect your Google account to enable calendar-aware scheduling. If you do, the App requests the following OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly — to read your calendar list and free/busy intervals
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create and delete work-session events on your behalf

These scopes are used for three specific purposes only:

  • Reading availability — the App queries the free/busy API to determine how many open hours you have each day. Only busy/free time intervals are read; event titles, descriptions, attendees, and other event details are never accessed or stored.
  • Writing work blocks — when you click “Add to GCal,” the App creates an event containing only the task name, a scheduled time slot, and a private internal tag so the App can later identify its own events.
  • Deleting work blocks — when you remove a scheduled block, the App deletes the corresponding event it previously created.
Google Calendar data is processed transiently in your browser only. No calendar event content, titles, or attendee information is ever sent to or stored on Commitments’ servers.

Your Google OAuth access token is cached in your browser’s localStorage and expires after approximately one hour. It is never transmitted to the App’s backend. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by clicking “Disconnect” within the App.

The App’s use of Google Calendar data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2d. Automatically Collected Technical Data

We do not operate our own analytics or logging pipeline. Supabase (database host) and Vercel (deployment host) may collect standard operational data such as IP addresses and request timestamps as part of their respective service operations. See their privacy policies in Section 5 below.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing the App’s core functionality — storing, retrieving, and displaying your tasks, categories, substeps, and planned schedule
  • Authentication — verifying your identity and maintaining your session
  • Calendar integration — reading your free/busy intervals and writing work-session events on your explicit request
  • Personalization — saving your display preferences so they persist across sessions

We do not use your data to train machine-learning models, build advertising profiles, or sell to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

4a. Supabase (Server-Side)

Your account and planning data reside in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase in the us-east-1 (N. Virginia) AWS region. Every table in the database is protected by Row Level Security (RLS), meaning database queries are enforced at the row level so that each authenticated user can access only their own data. Supabase encrypts data at rest and in transit (TLS).

4b. Browser localStorage (Client-Side)

The following data is stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage and is never transmitted to Commitments servers:

ItemPurpose
Google OAuth access token & expiry Authenticating Google Calendar API calls from your browser
Google account hint (email) Pre-filling the Google sign-in prompt on re-authentication
Free/busy cache snapshot Displaying available time in the Planner without repeated API calls (stale after 1 hour)
Selected calendar IDs Remembering which of your calendars to include in availability queries
GCal scheduler settings Preserving your work-window, buffer, and efficiency preferences
Task-to-GCal-event registry Tracking which work blocks have been pushed to Google Calendar so they can be updated or deleted

This data is stored only on your device. Clearing your browser’s site data or using a different browser will remove it entirely.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Commitments does not use cookies, analytics services, advertising trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts. No data about your usage is shared with advertising platforms.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained in our database for as long as your account remains active. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records by contacting us via GitHub. Upon such a request, we will delete your records from the tasks, substeps, quick_tasks, categories, scheduled_days, user_preferences, and gcal_tokens tables within thirty (30) days.

8. Data Portability

You may export your task and schedule data at any time in .ics (iCalendar) format using the built-in export feature. The exported file includes your tasks’ due dates and scheduled work sessions and can be imported into any standard calendar application.

9. Children’s Privacy

Commitments is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so that we may delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App’s features or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the “Last Revised” date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
